## Repository authority

This extension owns LV project extraction/reconciliation, the canonical project model, LV implementation and channel/grouping rules, spatial conventions, validation, markup conventions, and generators. General electrical lighting design knowledge remains in WikiJS. Follow the [full workflow and readiness map](docs/design-playbooks/lv-project-workflow-and-readiness.md). Read the [boundary](docs/governance-and-doctrine/repository-boundary.md), [page registry](docs/governance-and-doctrine/page-type-registry.md), [playbook](docs/design-playbooks/lighting-design-playbook.md), and [model overview](docs/ontology/canonical-model/README.md) before changing these interfaces.

Knowledge structure follows meaning; workflow lives in playbooks. Do not import BAS I/O, PLC/controller, or controls-narrative ontology unless a specific cross-domain need is established.


**Phase 1 execution order:** Follow [Phase 1 Intake Process Map v0.1](docs/design-playbooks/phase-1-intake-process-map-v0.1.md). Preliminary light-point observation may precede initial room polygons solely for geometric containment; it does not assign final Light IDs or replace architectural-first Space source authority. After reviewed Space association, M1 names Light Points in **one continuous project-wide sequence**: order rooms top-left to bottom-right in a row-by-row grid, then within each room number its light points top-left to bottom-right in the same row-by-row manner. **Never restart numbering** when entering another room, level, or fixture type. Preserve immutable source observation/object IDs. By default the register `label` **equals the Light Point name**, except for an explicit owner override. Approved M1 names and labels carry unchanged into M2; no second labeling scheme. Owner-first markup intake and repeated accepted-geometry-preserving PDF reconciliation are supported process paths. Full fixture schedule-first reconciliation remains the M2 gate.

**Standard lighting sequences and zone endpoints:** The parent [SEQ Registry](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/design-playbooks/lighting-control-standard-sequences.md) owns framework `SEQ-###` definitions; reserved drafts are not approved for auto-selection. M3 matches verified project room/energy-code requirements and source MEP intent to approved standard revisions or owner-created project sequences (e.g. `PSEQ-###`). **Associate a sequence only with an endpoint/leaf lighting zone, never a parent**. Preserve the v0.7 parent aggregation contract and keep daylight overlays distinct from exclusive tree membership. Current LV model does not yet implement validated sequence references; record interim M3 evidence without unversioned schema changes.

**M3 executable contract:** Follow the [LV M3 Sequence Assignment Companion v0.1](docs/ontology/canonical-model/m3-sequence-assignments-v0.1.md) and parent [M3 lifecycle](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/design-playbooks/m3-lighting-sequence-lifecycle-v0.1.md). Store selected approved standard `SEQ-` or owner-created project `PSEQ-` revision on **leaf endpoint zone assignments only**. Use the versioned companion rather than adding unknown fields to v0.7-v0.9. Match requirements and source intent before adoption; verify daylight and every Space; generate grouped M3 narrative HTML/CSV review. `SEQ-001` through `SEQ-007` are reserved drafts, **not** approved recipes.

**Dimming contract:** M3 sequence definitions explicitly identify required `dimming_scheme`: `continuous`, `stepped`, or `none` (with `unknown` only while unresolved); stepped needs number of steps. Use [M3 companion v0.2](schemas/m3-sequence-assignments-v0.2.schema.json). Source fixtures capture a separate controlled enum `dimming_interfaces[]` in the [fixture dimming companion](schemas/fixture-dimming-interface-v0.1.schema.json), with phase-cut, 0–10 V, 1–10 V, DALI, DMX, PWM, CCR, wireless-integrated, none/unknown/other choices plus min dim percent, dim-to-off, analog sink/source mode, driver location, and evidence. `source_dimming_capability`, driver architecture, electrical CV/CC and interface are independent. Export `fixture_type_schedule.csv` with `--dimming-register` rather than inventing values; M4 must validate selected dimmer/controller output compatibility separately. See [shared vocabulary](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/ontology/lighting-dimming-vocabulary-v0.1.md).

## Engineering invariants

Start room extraction with the Architectural Floor Plan or Dimension Plan and follow [architectural space intake](docs/design-playbooks/architectural-space-intake.md). Compare architectural floor, architectural RCP, and electrical room labels; record mismatches in the discrepancy list. Assign lights by their primary served Space/occupied level, preserve the fixture source sheet separately, and put mounting height/basis on the Light Object. Describe consequential partial-wall/open boundaries and request review before finalizing uncertain zoning; do not require a wall object catalog. Include unnumbered chases/service voids with location/purpose notes. Preserve the MEP stair-control scheme. One shared zone per physical stair is a reviewed implementation convention only when source behavior and independent-control constraints support it; retain one Space or separate level records without duplicating lights.

Apply [Milestone 1](docs/design-playbooks/architectural-space-intake.md#milestone-1-room-inventory-and-source-verification): independently verify the simple room list first, flag omissions for owner review and cause analysis, then verify other source facts. No assumptions are permitted in asserted source facts; each fact needs source evidence or identified owner confirmation, and unsupported values stay unknown. For boundary review, follow walls for enclosed rooms and draw visibly provisional logical-area polygons using architectural use/circulation, partial walls, finish/material transitions, features and ceiling evidence. Approximate geometry is permitted for owner editing, without inventing source names, room use or code/control conclusions. Follow the [annotation naming and identity convention](docs/design-playbooks/architectural-space-intake.md#annotation-type-naming-and-identity): room name in Subject, Space ID/basis in Comments, actual author, and a persistent annotation ID mapped to the Space. Capture clear architectural area labels with high confidence. Prefer floor/dimension-plan boundary markups, otherwise the applicable RCP, and use the printed view scale as the preferred dimension source. Preserve editable, unflattened annotations and Space IDs through the owner's PDF correction/return loop. Native Bluebeam Area output requires a successful compatibility test before being promised. Apply the untagged-area rules: an untagged door alcove belongs to its surrounding Space; a traffic corridor is its own proposed tracking Space; finish transitions can split corridor tracking. Fixture layout never sets a boundary. Inspect finish plans and confirm substantial shaded/hatched scope exclusions during registration/intake; excluded backgrounds need only a retained scope note. Verify saved raw annotation IDs and a human edit/save/readback, not an unchanged-file round trip.

For current work, read [Physical intake v0.4](docs/ontology/canonical-model/physical-intake-v0.4.md) and its [v0.2 device hierarchy](docs/ontology/canonical-model/device-hierarchy-v0.2.md). Start with architectural room facts and extract the specified MEP lighting/control scheme from all relevant plans, legends, schedules, notes, and details. Check that scheme through WikiJS using the reviewed energy-code basis; preserve source intent separately from findings and LV implementation. Do not invent controls to fill missing information. Group channels within verified electrical limits while preserving source/approved operation. Redesign only with established authority and documented approval of the departure. Building Code Space Type, Building Code Occupancy Group, and Energy Code Space Type are independent fields; preserve unknowns and classification decisions. Store physical lights once in root `light_objects[]`; every light must have exactly one primary served Space after boundary derivation/counting. This is a primary intake sanity check. Zones later reference lights by ID. Create no default/placeholder zones during room or fixture intake; empty zone lists are valid. Establish functional zones only from supported control intent/reviewed decisions, and flag missing assignments at the later design phase.

The owner has superseded the v0.1 one-zone-per-power-channel restriction: v0.4 separates physical light ownership from later zone ID lists, each single-input light references one power channel, and many-to-many channel/zone views are derived. Branch circuits own power units and power units own channels. Controller output identity is separate from supply-channel identity. Preserve shared source schedule groups, normal operating state, emergency force-on inputs, and separate backup power provisions. Use class-specific output profiles rather than globally imposing 100/90 W or 0-56 V limits. The original invariants below describe the narrower v0.1 tools, which remain available until migration is complete.

Use WikiJS for general room/control design knowledge. General [application-guide](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/design-playbooks/lighting-control-application-guide.md) and [IECC draft profiles](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/design-playbooks/iecc-occupant-sensor-directives.md) now live upstream; the legacy LV pages are pointers only. Follow the [classification/narrative handoff](docs/design-playbooks/lv-project-workflow-and-readiness.md#room-classification-and-controls-narrative-handoff) and [WikiJS review playbook](https://github.com/kissolutions/knowledgebase_wikijs/blob/main/design-playbooks/room-classification-and-controls-review-playbook.md): propose synonym-based independent room classifications, record owner confirmation, extract controls by function, and obtain scoped permission or owner narrative before filling source gaps. Incomplete WikiJS guidance triggers selected-code primary-source review; derivations/options and the completed narrative need approval before adoption. Room-type confirmation, permission to draft and approval to adopt are distinct. Missing guidance does not prove source deficiency or compliance. Local LV constraints narrow compatible selections without weakening applicable requirements. Apply supported guide/code rules as checks on extracted MEP intent; use them to develop alternatives only when redesign is authorized. Keep independent control zones, physical sensor counts, and supply channels distinct. Do not infer adoption, room use, exceptions, or coverage. The owner-agreed zone verification and implementation-review statuses are documented in the [workflow map](docs/design-playbooks/lv-project-workflow-and-readiness.md#control-review-results) but are not yet schema fields. Retain findings/evidence in project review records until a versioned extension is implemented.

- Facts from sources, assumptions, engineering decisions, and generated presentation remain distinguishable.
- Every source fact and consequential selection retains source references. Source revisions are explicit; never silently overwrite a fact when a plan changes.
- Historical v0.1 nominal 100 W channel baseline: design load <= 90 W; current M4 uses <=95 W, with any lower verified equipment limit honored. This is KIS design doctrine, not a universal code/listing claim.
- Calculate with confirmed LV input load at the channel interface. Do not automatically reuse original AC fixture wattage or assume driver compatibility.
- Legacy v0.1: one explicit scope disposition per physical fixture; one channel per included fixture; no assignments to excluded fixtures.
- Legacy v0.1: one required room/micro-zone per channel. Independent daylight, manual, occupancy, dimming, or other source control boundaries survive the transformation.
- Unknown values are `null`, not zero. Create open items and mark decisions provisional; final grouping requires resolved blocking uncertainty.
- Source/control departures require established authority, approval and traceable decisions while preserving the observed intent. Legacy v0.1 uses `zone_assignment`; do not insert that field into v0.3. A confirmed decision alone does not establish external approval.
- Manufacturer electrical limits, output/driver type, aggregate power, and channel capacity require verified evidence and engineering review.

For M2 fixture scheduling, follow [M2 Approved Light Point Identity and Fixture Scheduling](docs/design-playbooks/m2-fixture-numbering.md): consume the latest **approved M1 Light Point Register and its labels**, retaining the uninterrupted project-wide Light Point naming sequence (rooms in grid order; points in each room row-by-row top-left to bottom-right), independent of fixture type; register labels match Light Point names unless expressly overridden by the owner. M2 enriches the same objects with exact source schedule types, evidence, electrical facts and physical occurrence/assembly reconciliation; **never renumber by fixture type blocks or clockwise rings**. Keep immutable Light Object `id`, source observation IDs, and accepted PDF annotation references separate from the display `label`. Changes arising from new/missing/split/merged points become explicit findings and owner-reviewed label/association changes, not silent type-based reallocation. The exporter displays entered labels but does not certify M1 approval or automate these reconciliation decisions.

For linear M2 extraction, follow [source-run counting](docs/design-playbooks/m2-linear-fixture-extraction.md): one continuous/apparently continuous same-type path counts once through corners or repeated labels; clearly disconnected paths are separate runs. Inspect original linework beneath overlays and distinguish ceiling/detail annotations from fixture marks. Flag continuity and length/load/topology uncertainty. A source-run count does not establish one manufactured section, electrical feed or channel; retain stable identity and avoid duplicating full paths/loads to bypass current single-input modeling.

Current electrical extension: [v0.5](docs/ontology/canonical-model/electrical-interfaces-v0.5.md), retaining v0.4 physical-first ownership. Capture source voltage as a peer of source wattage, with AC/DC, nominal/range, CV/CC/current evidence and explicit interface basis. Preserve it separately from selected light input/channel output. Confirm fixed-voltage and mode compatibility independently of watts/group labels; verify single-input CC current/compliance ranges and flag multi-input CC topology. Do not infer electrical values during upgrade or assume controller count from watt totals. v0.5 checker/exporter support is implemented; older schemas/tools remain available without claiming the new checks.

Record source driver/driverless/other architecture and its note separately from the selected LV architecture. Other requires an explanatory description; unknown remains null. CV/CC regulation, dimming and legacy zone `driver_type` are different meanings. Unknown selected driver architecture is a design blocker; do not infer it from source description, watts or a zone's dimming label.

## Milestone Markup and Device Review

All markup work remains live, editable and unflattened at every stage, including draft, accepted, issued, final coordinated and closeout packages. Review completion or narrative lock never authorizes flattening. Preserve editable labels, symbols, room fills/boundaries, zones, channels, equipment and cabling, with stable annotation IDs and linked object metadata. Do not rasterize markups, bake them into page content, or use a print-to-PDF path that destroys annotations. Assemble schedule front sheets and plan PDFs without flattening the plan annotations; source drawing backgrounds and ordinary schedule page content need not be converted into annotations. Verify annotation retention and editability in the actual saved/assembled deliverable; layers alone do not prove editability. Do not lock annotations against owner edits.

After room boundaries are accepted, retain fillable closed shapes or redraw nonfillable footprints as editable polygons with reconciled Space/annotation identity. Follow the milestone room-map legend: ordinary enclosed rooms blue; circulation green; open activity/common areas ruddy orange; multi-level atriums/open-to-above areas violet; service voids and confirmed out-of-scope/no-lighting areas gray; exterior areas low-opacity wine/burgundy. Use lighter low-opacity fills than outlines, preserve source visibility, and avoid duplicate area/level records. Presentation categories do not establish code classifications or device colors.

Follow [six milestone review packages](docs/design-playbooks/milestone-review-packages.md). Required room-device scheduling follows narrative lock. Apply device-specific provisional placement: wall switches/scene controllers/dimmers first use electrical lighting-plan locations, preserving accepted owner changes; only absent usable source locations use latch-side door frames, glazing/adjacent-wall rules or open-area entry routes; occupancy and daylight sensors also start at source design locations, preserving accepted owner changes. Only without usable source locations apply ceiling room/child-zone centers (tile centers when visible), wall occupancy top-left placeholders, corridor midpoints or flagged daylight placeholders. Do not automatically recenter a source-located sensor. Follow the milestone playbook for glazing/swing exceptions and unknown-geometry fallbacks. Owner-returned locations guide subsequent aggregation/routing. Use v0.7 parent/child zone records, topology v1.1 device identity, and markup manifest v1. Calculated display labels round upward to whole numbers without changing model/check precision. Render text last/in front. Enveloped zones use dashed boundaries preserving other style; avoid polyline detours. Takeoff rooms are lightly hatched; micro-channel maps exclude devices. Final layers: rooms, lights, zones, lv_channels, controllers, cabling. Never claim PDF layers/edit-readback from CSV/HTML or schema checks. No build is required to copy framework updates.

## Larger-Fixture EPS Power Aggregation

A fixture above 95 W may be a valid design candidate using the owner-directed EPS fixture power aggregator: 1–4 separate Class 2 inputs, one combined output up to an owner-stated 400 W, inside the fixture housing. Reserve it for on/off-only nominal 48 VDC CV through EPS outputs; not dimming/CC. Scale feeds to actual fixture demand while honoring verified limits and project margins; preserve one physical light, explicit feed identities and common switching. Do not treat it as a sensor aggregator, a QDCD with four outputs or an ordinary 400 W Class 2 channel. Follow the [allocation directive and model-support gate](docs/design-playbooks/controller-power-placement.md#eps-fixture-power-aggregation--owner-directive-2026-10-06). Current single-channel light and topology feed checks do not support this path; record the design candidate and feed ledger without falsely claiming validation.

Oversized single-fixture branch starts at **>95 W**. Dimming requirements hard-block the typical EPS fixture-aggregator path. Compatible on/off 48 VDC CV uses one dedicated combined-power fixture micro zone with 1–4 upstream feeds and one output; preserve one full-load label and functional-zone association. Refer to the allocation decision tree for the 95–100 W interval, owner-described 100 W < load < 400 W band and nominal maximum review.

Extract the original MEP fixture schedule as referenced design information before accepting fixture counts and type/linear continuity interpretations. Preserve exact type suffixes, descriptions, geometry/section clues, source watts/voltage/driver facts and note/detail locators separately from selected LV replacements. Reconcile the schedule with RCP paths and electrical symbols/circuiting/daisy chaining. Missing schedules must be retrieved if referenced; absent design-build schedules are recorded without fabrication, with supported takeoff provisional where unresolved.
