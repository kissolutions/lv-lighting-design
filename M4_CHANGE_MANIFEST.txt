Key decisions encoded:
- AC/DC is a peer power-type attribute; voltage stays numeric.
- nominal/min/max voltage remain explicit numeric attributes.
- physical fixture dimming capability is separate from zone-applied dimming behavior.
- dimmable fixture in non-dimming zone is valid and does not mutate physical electrical data.
- non-dimmable fixture in dimming-required zone is blocked.
- micro LV channel serves one functional zone; one zone may use many channels.
- physical controller/output claims are 1:1 with micro LV channels when assigned.
- new M4 Class 2 profile is 100 W rated / 95 W max design load.
- legacy v0.5 and 90 W profile are retained rather than overwritten.
